Evolutionary Individuality: Human-Readable Companion to the Domain Adapter / Contract
For the Evolutionary Individuality Domain Adapter / Contract and Structural Flow Universal Kernel v1.0
1. Purpose
This companion explains the human-readable meaning of the Evolutionary Individuality Domain Adapter / Contract and its interface with the Structural Flow Universal Kernel v1.0. The adapter machine-encodes the formal structure of the proposed experiment developed in Evolutionary Individuality as an Empirical Question: A Proposed Experimental Test in Pseudomonas. The scientific paper supplies the biological object, experimental contrast, evidence, account commitments, outcome logic, and limits of inference; the adapter receives those commitments only after they have been adjudicated into formal states.
The companion sits beside the Lean adapter. Its job is to make the formal package auditable: what its objects and burdens mean, how DISCHARGED / VIOLATED / OPEN are used, how the three accounts route to native verdicts, how normalization and cross-seat comparison work, what the two encoded witnesses demonstrate, and what the checkpoint theorem closes. It may refer to the scientific paper when a formal declaration needs biological interpretation, but it is not a second scientific argument.
2. Package Position
The package contains four non-substitutable components.
2.1 Universal Kernel
Structural Flow Universal Kernel v1.0 supplies the universal translation and adjudication machinery used by the adapter through StructuralFlow.UniversalTranslationContract. The kernel distinguishes structural admissibility from downstream domain outcomes. This adapter required no kernel modification.
2.2 Scientific Paper
Evolutionary Individuality as an Empirical Question supplies the scientific basis for the formalization. It defines the genealogically traceable WS↔SM collective lineage, metapopulation treatment structure, Non-Mixed versus Mixed propagule contrast, post-evolution scaffold challenge, experimental horizons, common-environment re-assay, comparator accounts, and scientific inference limits. Lean does not generate or validate those premises.
2.3 Domain Adapter / Contract
Evolutionary_Individuality_Domain_Adapter_Contract_v0_2.lean, in namespace StructuralFlow.EvolutionaryIndividuality, is the machine-readable domain adapter and contract. It declares object burdens, domain-entry burdens, account-specific downstream burdens, native verdict functions, normalization, cross-seat routing, two fixed witness states, and a checkpoint theorem. It also exposes an experimenter-facing adjudication record and report surface through which results from the proposed experiment can be entered after scientific adjudication. It routes already-adjudicated states; it does not infer them from raw data.
2.4 Human-Readable Companion
This companion explains the machine-readable adapter / contract and its kernel interface. The package contains four non-substitutable components:
1. the scientific paper defines the biological experiment, evidence requirements, and intended scientific interpretation;
2. the Lean adapter / contract defines and machine-encodes the domain burdens, adjudication rules, experimenter-input surface, and account-routing logic;
3. the universal kernel supplies the general formal interface used by the adapter / contract; and
4. this companion explains the machine file and its relationship to the scientific paper and kernel.
No component substitutes for the others.
3. What Machine Closure Means
The v0.2 adapter / contract was tested with Structural Flow Universal Kernel v1.0 and returned no messages. That establishes conditional machine closure: given the encoded object commitments, entry states, and downstream states, Lean accepts the declared routing to native verdicts, normalized verdicts, cross-seat results, and the structural distinctions used by the compatible kernel.
It does not establish that the experiment has been performed or that any empirical burden has actually been discharged, violated, or left open. Those remain scientific judgments supplied to the adapter / contract after the experimental evidence has been interpreted.
3.1 Experimenter Execution Surface
The v0.2 adapter / contract provides an explicit input surface for specialists who have run the proposed experiment. After the resulting evidence has been scientifically adjudicated, the experimenter enters the resulting states into:
submittedExperiment : ExperimentAdjudication
The record contains the two object commitments, ten domain-entry requirements, and twelve downstream account requirements. Each is assigned one of the inherited dispositions:
discharged
violated
open
The public file ships with every field set to open. These values are placeholders, not empirical claims. A field should be changed only after the corresponding requirement has been adjudicated from the evidence described in the scientific paper.
The primary execution command is:
#eval experimentReport submittedExperiment
The report first evaluates the structural admission surface. Its translationStatus is:
structuralConforms when the required object and entry states are discharged;
noLawfulTranslation when at least one required object or entry state is violated; or
notYetAdjudicable when no required object or entry state is violated but at least one remains open.
Account-level outputs are returned only after structural admission passes. A structurally conforming submission then reports whether downstream burdens are violated, open, or fully discharged; the native Clarke, Bourrat, and life-cycle verdicts; their normalized forms; and the resulting cross-seat verdict.
The same submission also generates:
experimentWorld submittedExperiment
which is the corresponding World ObjectBurden DomainBurden supplied to the universal kernel architecture. The universal core state is held discharged on this execution surface because the published adapter / contract already fixes the object class, level, scope, and translation being used. A materially different biological object, level, scope, or formal translation requires a new or re-audited adapter rather than silent reuse of the input record.
The two encoded witness states remain fixed regression cases. Experimenters enter results only in submittedExperiment; they do not alter the target-discordance or bounded-resilience witnesses.
4. The Kernel / Adapter Boundary
The kernel supplies universal translation machinery; the adapter supplies evolutionary-individuality-specific object, entry, and downstream content. Structural admissibility is therefore evaluated separately from downstream scientific success. A downstream burden may be VIOLATED or OPEN while the translated world remains StructuralConforms. That distinction is central to the two witnesses encoded later in the adapter.
5. Formal Object Commitments
Before the adapter can compare the three accounts, it requires a common biological object.
The Lean adapter declares two required object burdens:
collectiveLineageEntity
and
sharedSurfaceAcrossSeats.
5.1 collectiveLineageEntity
This burden pins the candidate biological object to the WS↔SM collective lineage considered as a life-cycle entity.
The formal object is not:
* an isolated WS mat;
* an isolated SM propagule pool;
* a culture vessel;
* an individual bacterial cell; or
* the entire experimental metapopulation.
The constituent particle level is the Pseudomonas fluorescens cell. The focal biological level is the collective lineage extending across its recurrent WS↔SM life-cycle organization.
The metapopulation has a different job. It is the experimental level at which the scaffold treatment is assigned and at which differences among candidate lineages can be compared. It is not substituted for the lineage-level candidate individual.
5.2 sharedSurfaceAcrossSeats
The second object burden requires the Clarke, Bourrat, and life-cycle-closure comparisons to refer to the same pinned lineage, level, and experimental scope.
The formal comparison would lose its meaning if one account were evaluated on the WS mat, another on the full metapopulation, and another on a genealogical lineage spanning several stages. Cross-account disagreement is informative only when the accounts are answering different questions about the same biological case.
The shared-object requirement therefore precedes account-level evaluation.
Genealogical continuity is sufficient for the lineage relations used by the contract. The adapter does not require strict numerical identity of one physical collective through every life-cycle stage. What must remain scientifically traceable is the parent–offspring and lineage relationship through which descendant collective stages and descendant collective instances arise.
These object commitments establish the common biological referent on which the rest of the domain contract operates.
6. Disposition Semantics
The adapter inherits the kernel type Disposition with three constructors:
discharged
violated
open
This companion renders them as DISCHARGED, VIOLATED, and OPEN.
DISCHARGED means adequate adjudicated evidence supports the requirement at the declared object, level, and scope. VIOLATED means adequate adjudicated evidence supports failure of that requirement. OPEN means the requirement applies but available evidence does not adequately support either discharge or violation.
These are not confidence scores. In particular:
OPEN must not silently become VIOLATED.
Nor should a demonstrated failure remain OPEN merely because the resulting downstream conclusion is inconvenient.
The same states perform different roles at different layers. Entry burdens determine whether the experimental case is adequately specified for account-level adjudication. Downstream burdens determine what an admitted case supports under the three comparator accounts. Failure of entry therefore must not be converted into a negative biological verdict.
7. Domain-Entry Requirements
The adapter declares ten domain-entry burdens:
Lean identifier
	Human-readable job
	accountVersionPinned
	Freeze the comparator account formulations before outcomes are interpreted.
	ancestryAdequacy
	Establish the genealogy needed for heredity, reproductive contribution, and lineage-continuation claims.
	assayAdequacy
	Ensure the measurements can discriminate the scientific states assigned to them.
	interventionIntegrity
	Ensure the perturbation corresponds to the declared protected-propagule-lineage contrast.
	treatmentUnitIntegrity
	Preserve the metapopulation as the causal treatment unit and avoid pseudoreplication from nested observations.
	referenceProvenance
	Establish an independently warranted pre-challenge history for the reference material without selecting it for a desired account outcome.
	commonEnvironmentAdequacy
	Permit a defensible distinction between treatment-environment performance and heritable retention or erosion.
	fullTrajectoryAdequacy
	Preserve biologically relevant successes, failures, collapse, and extinction rather than conditioning only on survivors.
	lifeCycleStageAdequacy
	Ensure the life-cycle stages are biologically warranted and not merely laboratory handling steps.
	lifeCycleTrajectoryAdequacy
	Ensure observed transitions are adequate to evaluate biological multiplication, reestablishment, and closure.
	Several distinctions require explicit protection.
interventionIntegrity treats Mixed versus Non-Mixed as a manipulation of the protected propagule-lineage relation as a whole; it does not isolate spatial separation from cross-lineage competition.
treatmentUnitIntegrity does not prescribe replication or a statistical model. It requires the evidence to respect the level at which the treatment is assigned.
commonEnvironmentAdequacy does not prescribe one assay environment. It requires a comparison capable of distinguishing inherited organization from context-dependent expression.
fullTrajectoryAdequacy and lifeCycleTrajectoryAdequacy are not duplicates. The first protects completeness of the experimental record; the second asks whether the biologically declared stage transitions are adequate for life-cycle inference.
8. Entry Before Account-Level Evaluation
All domain-entry burdens are DISCHARGED in both encoded witness states. If a required entry burden is OPEN, the relevant comparison is not yet adequately adjudicable. If an entry burden is VIOLATED, the declared case fails that admission condition. Neither result is automatically a negative verdict under Clarke, Bourrat, or the life-cycle account.
9. Downstream Account Requirements
After entry is discharged, the adapter evaluates twelve downstream burdens. These are account-specific scientific requirements, not further admission tests. Shared evidence can bear on several burdens without making those burdens equivalent.
9.1 Clarke Downstream Requirements
Lean identifier
	Human-readable question
	c1FocalSelectionCapacity
	Does the pinned collective lineage possess a non-zero capacity to respond to selection at the collective level?
	c2QualifyingIndividuatingMechanism
	Is there at least one qualifying objective policing OR demarcating mechanism?
	c3MechanismGrounding
	Does the qualifying mechanism actually ground the focal selection capacity rather than merely correlate with collective performance?
	c4NonArbitraryGrouping
	Is the focal grouping biologically objective rather than observer-defined or merely a cross-level by-product?
	Protected propagule-lineage structure is the primary candidate demarcating mechanism in the proposed experiment, but the adapter does not declare it qualifying merely because the experimenter manipulates it. C2 and C3 remain separate so that a biologically real feature need not automatically receive the proposed individuating function.
10. Bourrat Downstream Requirements
Lean identifier
	Human-readable question
	b1CollectivePhenotypicVariation
	Do collective lineages differ in relevant higher-level phenotypes?
	b2CollectiveFitnessDifferences
	Do collective lineages differ in focal-level fitness or reproductive contribution?
	b3CollectiveHeredity
	Are relevant collective differences transmitted or recurrent through parent–descendant lineage relations?
	b4ObjectiveIndividuation
	Are the higher-level Darwinian properties generated by objective individuation rather than observer-imposed grouping?
	b5InternalEndogenizedGeneration
	Has the organization generating those properties become sufficiently internal/endogenized rather than remaining dependent on external individuation?
	B1–B3 establish the relevant higher-level Darwinian antecedent. B4 distinguishes objective individuation from arbitrary grouping but does not require that the individuating cause be internal.
B5 carries an asymmetric inference in the proposed experiment. Adequate evidence that a required component of the organization remains dependent on protected propagule-lineage structure can support B5 = VIOLATED. Survival after relaxation of that one relation cannot by itself support B5 = DISCHARGED while other potentially load-bearing external contributions remain untested. In the adapter's bounded-resilience witness, that branch is B5 = OPEN.
11. Life-Cycle-Closure Downstream Requirements
Lean identifier
	Human-readable question
	l1Multiplication
	Do biological descendants establish a new focal collective-stage instance?
	l2Closure
	Does the prospectively declared biological stage graph form a connected cycle in which every stage can eventually be reached from every other stage?
	l3FocalStageReestablishment
	Are the focal biological stages recurrently regenerated across collective generations?
	These burdens are evaluated only after lifeCycleStageAdequacy and lifeCycleTrajectoryAdequacy are DISCHARGED. Researcher transfer between vessels does not itself constitute biological multiplication, and a repeating laboratory schedule does not itself establish closure.

18. Why the Two Witnesses Are Paired
The witnesses isolate one formal distinction while holding the remainder of the domain state fixed:
Target: B5 VIOLATED → darwinianLike → resolved discordant
Bounded resilience: B5 OPEN → Bourrat indeterminate → cross-seat indeterminate
Together they show that structural conformance can coexist with either a downstream violation or a downstream open burden, and that neither state is the same as structural-translation failure.

20. Scope of the Machine Result
The formal package is narrower than the experiment it represents. The adapter does not ingest raw data or determine whether ancestry, assays, mechanism claims, endogenization, or life-cycle closure are empirically adequate. Those judgments remain scientific inputs.
Machine closure does not establish:
* that the experiment has been performed;
* that any assay is biologically adequate;
* that any burden is empirically DISCHARGED, VIOLATED, or OPEN;
* that protected propagule-lineage structure is empirically a Clarke individuating mechanism;
* that B5 is empirically violated or discharged;
* that collective life-cycle closure is empirically present or absent;
* that one account of evolutionary individuality is correct; or
* that this application empirically validates the universal kernel.
It establishes only that, given the declared formal states, the adapter / contract routes them consistently through the compatible universal kernel. 
21. Relation to the Scientific Paper
Evolutionary Individuality as an Empirical Question carries the scientific argument: why the proposed experiment is informative, what evidence is required, how the comparator accounts differ, and what experimental outcomes would support.
The adapter and companion begin after those scientific judgments have been made. Their question is whether the consequences can be represented without changing the biological object, collapsing the accounts, erasing OPEN states, or confusing a downstream scientific result with failure of structural translation.
For the encoded target and bounded-resilience branches, the adapter answers that formal question affirmatively. No modification to Structural Flow Universal Kernel v1.0 was required.
